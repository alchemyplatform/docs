## Node

**Upcoming deprecations**

* **Botanix Mainnet and Testnet** (`botanix-mainnet`, `botanix-testnet`) — Alchemy support ends on **August 1, 2026**. The Botanix team is shutting down both networks. See the [Botanix Deprecation Notice](https://www.alchemy.com/docs/reference/botanix-deprecation-notice).
* **Polygon PoS Erigon client** — Polygon Labs is sunsetting the Erigon client on Polygon PoS on **August 1, 2026**. Alchemy is migrating Polygon Mainnet and Amoy from Erigon to Bor. After this date, the `trace_*` and `erigon_*` namespaces will no longer be available on `polygon-mainnet` or `polygon-amoy`, and Bor supports `eth_getProof` only for the latest 128 blocks. See the [Polygon PoS Erigon Client Deprecation Notice](https://www.alchemy.com/docs/reference/polygon-pos-erigon-deprecation-notice) for the full migration guide.
