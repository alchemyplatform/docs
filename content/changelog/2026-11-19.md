## Wallets

**Updates**

* Gas sponsorship is now available on Elysium Mainnet and Testnet. You can sponsor gas for your users with Wallet APIs, Bundler APIs, and Paymaster APIs on both networks, and Elysium chain definitions are available in `@alchemy/common/chains`. See [Wallet APIs supported chains](https://www.alchemy.com/docs/wallets/supported-chains).

## Data

**Updates**

* Added Tempo support to Data APIs and webhooks, so you can query indexed onchain data and receive activity notifications without building your own indexing infrastructure. See [Tempo APIs](https://www.alchemy.com/docs/tempo/tempo-api-overview).
