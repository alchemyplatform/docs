## Wallets

**SDK**

* [SDK v5 reference docs](https://www.alchemy.com/docs/wallets/reference/wallet-apis) are now live. We recommend that `aa-sdk` users upgrade. [`@alchemy/wallet-apis`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@alchemy/wallet-apis) is the recommended package for most users, with updated lower-level packages available for integrating directly with viem.
* Added stricter selector checks in the MAv2 permission builder.

**Rundler**

* Fixed an initialization bug with flashblocks on fast block time networks (e.g., Xlayer).
