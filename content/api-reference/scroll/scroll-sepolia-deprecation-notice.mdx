## ⚠️ Deprecation Notice

**Alchemy will fully deprecate support for the Scroll Sepolia testnet on July 30, 2026.** After this date, requests to Scroll Sepolia endpoints (`scroll-sepolia`) will no longer be supported.

Alchemy is ending support because the Scroll team is shutting down the Scroll Sepolia testnet. Scroll Mainnet remains fully supported.

## What you need to do

There is no direct testnet replacement, as the Scroll Sepolia testnet itself is being shut down by the Scroll team. Before July 30, 2026:

1. Remove any dependencies on Scroll Sepolia endpoints (`scroll-sepolia`) from your applications.
2. If you need a testnet environment for development, explore Alchemy's other [supported chains](/docs/reference/node-supported-chains) for an alternative testnet that fits your needs, or move production traffic to **Scroll Mainnet**:

```text
https://scroll-mainnet.g.alchemy.com/v2/YOUR_ALCHEMY_API_KEY
```
