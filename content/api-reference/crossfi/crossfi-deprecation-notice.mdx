## ⚠️ Deprecation Notice

**Alchemy support for CrossFi Mainnet (`crossfi-mainnet`) and CrossFi Testnet (`crossfi-testnet`) ended on November 26, 2026.** Requests to these endpoints are no longer supported.

## What you need to do

1. Remove any dependencies on the `crossfi-mainnet` and `crossfi-testnet` endpoints from your applications.
2. If you are looking for a new EVM-compatible network to build on, explore Alchemy's other [supported chains](/docs/reference/node-supported-chains) for an alternative that fits your needs.

## Need help?

If you have any questions or need assistance, contact us at support@alchemy.com or open a ticket in the [Alchemy Dashboard](https://dashboard.alchemy.com/).
