## Overview

Starting January 1, 2027, Alchemy will charge sales tax on invoices where required by law. Many US states require providers of software and digital services to collect sales tax from their customers, and we're updating our billing to meet those requirements.

This page covers when sales tax starts, how it's applied, what you'll see on your invoice, and what you need to do.

## When sales tax starts

Sales tax applies to invoices issued on or after **January 1, 2027**. Invoices issued before that date aren't affected.

## How sales tax is applied

Sales tax is based on the **billing address** on your account.

* **Rates vary by location.** The rate depends on your state and local jurisdiction.
* **Your plan price doesn't change.** Your plan price and usage rates stay the same. Sales tax is added on top where it applies.
* **Payment method doesn't matter.** Sales tax is part of the invoice total, regardless of how you pay, including crypto.

## What to expect on your invoice

When tax applies, it shows as a separate line on your invoice.

## Keep your billing address up to date

We use your billing address to calculate tax on every invoice, so make sure it's complete and accurate.

To check or update it:

1. Open the Alchemy Dashboard and go to **Settings**.
2. Select **Billing**.
3. Update your address in the **Billing details** section.
