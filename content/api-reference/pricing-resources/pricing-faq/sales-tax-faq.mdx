Starting January 1, 2027, Alchemy will charge sales tax on invoices where required by law. This FAQ explains what changes, who it affects, and what you need to do.

1. **Why is Alchemy charging sales tax?**

   Many US states require providers of software and digital services to collect sales tax from their customers. We're updating our billing to meet those requirements.

2. **When does this start?**

   Sales tax applies to invoices issued on or after January 1, 2027. Invoices issued before that date aren't affected.

4. **How is the tax amount calculated?**

   Tax is based on the billing address on your account. The rate depends on your state and local jurisdiction, so it varies by location. When tax applies, it shows as a separate line on your invoice.

5. **Does my plan price change?**

   No. Your plan price and usage rates stay the same. Sales tax is added on top where it applies.

6. **How do I check or update my billing address?**

   In the Alchemy Dashboard, go to **Settings > Billing** and update the **Billing details** section. Make sure the address is complete and accurate, since we use it to calculate tax on every invoice.
