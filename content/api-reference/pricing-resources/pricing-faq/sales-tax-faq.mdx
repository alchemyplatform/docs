## Overview

Many US states require providers of software and digital services to collect sales tax from their customers, and we're updating our billing to meet those requirements.

## When sales tax starts

Sales tax applies to invoices issued on or after **January 1, 2027**. Invoices issued before that date aren't affected.

## Who is affected

Sales tax applies to customers with a billing address in a US state or locality where Alchemy is required to collect it.

## How sales tax is applied

Sales tax is based on the **billing address** on your account.

* **Rates vary** by state and local jurisdiction.
* Sales tax is **added on top** of your plan price and usage rates, which don't change.
* Sales tax is part of the invoice total **regardless of how you pay**, including crypto.

## What to expect on your invoice

When tax applies, it shows as a separate line on your invoice.

## Keep your billing address up to date

We use your billing address to calculate tax on every invoice, so make sure it's complete and accurate.

To check or update it:

1. Open the Alchemy Dashboard and go to **Settings**.
2. Select **Billing**.
3. Update your address in the **Billing details** section.

### Missing or invalid billing address

We need a complete, valid billing address to issue your invoice. Add or update it before January 1, 2027.

## Tax-exempt organizations

To claim an exemption, email your exemption certificate to [billing@alchemy.com](mailto:billing@alchemy.com) with the subject “Sales tax exemption” and include your account email and organization name. The certificate needs to cover the state on your billing address. After we verify it, we’ll stop charging sales tax on your future invoices. If you were charged tax before your exemption was verified, email [billing@alchemy.com](mailto:billing@alchemy.com) with the subject “Sales tax refund” and list the invoice numbers, and we’ll review them.

## Customers outside the US

No. This change applies to US sales tax only.

## Questions

Email [billing@alchemy.com](mailto:billing@alchemy.com). We can't give tax advice for your specific situation. If you have questions about how this affects your business, check with your own tax advisor.
