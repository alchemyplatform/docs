## Throughput (CUPS)

Each application has reserved dedicated throughput, measured in Compute Units per Second (CUPS). Your app can exceed these limits based on elastic demand in our system.

| Tier       | Base CUPS |
| ---------- | --------- |
| Free       | 300       |
| PAYG       | 10,000    |
| Enterprise | Custom    |

*Note: Even if your application limit is lower, the system may allow higher throughput based on available capacity. For a more robust experience, we recommend implementing retries when experiencing throughput errors.*

## Add-ons

| UTXO       | No   | Yes  | Included   |

### How add-ons are billed on PAYG

* **Buying mid-month:** You pay a prorated amount for the rest of the month, on a separate invoice the day you buy. The amount is the monthly price × the days left in the month, including the day you buy, ÷ the days in the month.
* **Renewal:** On the 1st of each month, the add-on is billed in advance for the new month, on the same invoice as the previous month's usage.
* **Removing or reducing:** The change takes effect on the 1st of the next month. You keep the add-on until then, and the current month isn't refunded.

For example, a $100/month add-on bought on October 10 costs $100 × 22 ÷ 31 = $70.97 on an October 10 invoice. Your November 1 invoice then includes $100 for November, plus your October usage.
