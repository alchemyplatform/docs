Scammers airdrop worthless tokens and collectibles to many addresses at once. They often copy the name of a real token, or link to a phishing site that drains wallets. Showing them to users clutters their portfolio and puts them at risk.

## How we flag spam

We classify token contracts and collections as spam using signals such as:

* Mass airdrops to unrelated addresses.
* Names or symbols copied from well-known tokens.
* Links to known phishing sites in names or metadata.
* No real trading or transfers apart from the airdrop.

## In responses

Spam is left out of Balances, Collectibles and Activity by default. To see it, for example to let users review hidden items, pass `include_spam=true`. Each item has a `spam` field, which is `true` for flagged items.

<Warning>
No filter is perfect. Never ask users to interact with a token just because it isn't flagged as spam.
</Warning>

Think a token is flagged by mistake, or missed? [Contact us](mailto:support@alchemy.com) with the contract address and network.
