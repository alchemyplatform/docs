* **Your keys.** Keys leaked to public GitHub repositories are [revoked automatically](/docs/documentation/account/security/leaked-key-detection), and tokens can be locked to your [IPs](/docs/how-to-add-allowlists-to-your-apps-for-enhanced-security) or [domains](/docs/how-to-add-allowlists-to-your-apps-for-enhanced-security).
* **Your team.** [Roles](/docs/dashboard-roles), [SSO](/docs/dashboard-sso) and [Activity Logs](/docs/activity-log) control and record who does what.
* **Our controls.** We're SOC 2 Type II audited, with yearly penetration tests and a bug bounty. See [Audits](/docs/documentation/account/security/audits).
