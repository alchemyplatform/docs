On Ethereum, a block becomes [`safe`](/docs/documentation/architecture/engine/consistency/commitment-levels) after about 6 minutes and `finalized` after about 13. The [Fast Confirmation Rule](https://fastconfirmation.it) (FCR) lets a block be confirmed within a few slots, about 30 seconds, as long as the network is running normally.

FCR is computed by the consensus client from validator votes. A confirmed block won't be reorged unless the network has a major outage or an attack.

## When to use it

* Bridges and cross-chain messaging that wait on Ethereum confirmations.
* Deposits and payments that need confidence faster than `safe`.

Keep using `finalized` when you need a guarantee that holds even during an outage.

## Availability

FCR is in early access on Ethereum mainnet and Sepolia. [Contact us](mailto:support@alchemy.com) to enable it.
