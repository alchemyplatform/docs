Teams group members, such as one per product or environment. A team can have many members, and a member can be on several teams. Each team has one or more [apps](/docs/create-an-api-key), and each app belongs to one team.

On the Enterprise plan, teams belong to an [organization](/docs/dashboard-organizations), and a member can have a different [role](/docs/dashboard-roles) on each team.
