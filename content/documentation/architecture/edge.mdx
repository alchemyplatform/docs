The edge is the front door to every request. It runs in each [region](/docs/documentation/architecture/regions), so your request is received close to where your app runs, and it handles everything that doesn't need a node.

## What the edge does

* **Receives your request** in the closest region. If a region has a problem, traffic moves to another one.
* **Checks your access token** and its [permissions](/docs/documentation/account/authentication/permissions), and rejects requests that aren't allowed before they reach the engine.
* **Applies your [throughput](/docs/reference/throughput) limit,** so a burst returns `429` quickly instead of slowing down other requests.
* **Gives the request a [trace ID](/docs/documentation/monitoring/tracing)** in `x-alchemy-trace-id`, or keeps the one you sent, so the same ID follows it through every layer.
* **Passes it to the [engine](/docs/documentation/architecture/engine/routing)**, which decides how to answer it.

## Caching

The edge works like a globally distributed cache: answers that can't change are served from locations around the world, beyond our [regions](/docs/documentation/architecture/regions#beyond-our-regions), without going further.
