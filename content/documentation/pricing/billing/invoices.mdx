## Pay as You Go

You're billed monthly for the compute units you used, and your card on file is charged automatically.

* **End of month:** you get an invoice for the month's usage.
* **During the month:** if your spend reaches a billing threshold before the month ends, we invoice what you've used so far. The end-of-month invoice then covers only the rest.
* **Credits** are used up before your card is charged.

<Note>
When you add a card, we place a temporary \$5 hold to verify it. It isn't a charge, and your bank releases it within about a week.
</Note>

### If a payment fails

We retry the payment automatically. After 3 failed attempts, your account is moved to the Free plan until the invoice is paid. Pay an unpaid invoice from the Billing page in the dashboard, or [update your payment method](/docs/documentation/pricing/billing/payment-methods).

## Enterprise

You're billed as set out in your contract:

* **Contract invoices** for your committed plan, on your contract's schedule.
* **Usage invoices** for anything you use beyond your committed amount, billed monthly.

Invoices are due within your contract's payment terms, such as 30 days. If an invoice stays unpaid, we'll send reminders before any change to your service.
