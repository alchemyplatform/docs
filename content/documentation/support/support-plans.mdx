## Business Hours

Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm Pacific Time.

## Channels

| Plan | Channels |
| - | - |
| Free |  Community |
| Pay as You Go |  Email |
| Enterprise |  Email, plus a dedicated  Slack or  Telegram channel |

## Response Targets

Time to First Response is how long until a person from our team replies. Automated replies don't count.

### By plan

| Plan | Time to First Response | Time to Resolution |
| - | - | - |
| Free | Best effort | — |
| Pay as You Go | 24h | — |
| Enterprise | By severity, see below | — |

### Enterprise, by severity

| Severity | Time to First Response | Time to Resolution |
| - | - | - |
| **Critical**: production down, no workaround | 1 business hour, or 3 hours outside business hours | — |
| **High**: degraded or intermittent service | 6 business hours | — |
| **Normal**: questions, feature requests, staging issues | 8 business hours | — |

Need faster responses? See the [Support add-on](/docs/documentation/pricing/add-ons).
