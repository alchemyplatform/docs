See live latency and success rates on [alchemy.com/benchmarks](https://www.alchemy.com/benchmarks).

The benchmarks compare Alchemy with other RPC providers on common read methods, such as balance lookups, block reads, log queries and receipt lookups. They run from US East, US West, EU Central and AP Southeast, on networks including Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum and BNB Smart Chain.

Results cover the latest 24 hours and refresh every 5 minutes.
