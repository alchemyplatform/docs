We're part of the community, not a vendor sitting on top of it. We run software we don't ship, so the success of the teams behind it is our reliability. Sponsoring follows from that.

## What we sponsor

* **Teams that grow the space.** We back client teams, chains and builders whose work makes the whole ecosystem bigger.
* **Client summits.** We organize or sponsor Ethereum client summits that bring client teams together.
* **Projects we want to see exist.** We put money behind them, not just words.

## How we decide

We sponsor work that makes the ecosystem better for everyone who builds on it, not just for Alchemy customers. If your team fits, [contact us](mailto:support@alchemy.com).

## Alchemy Ventures

Separate from sponsorship, Alchemy Ventures invests in early teams building the future of web3. See [every team we've funded since 2022](https://www.alchemy.com/ventures).
