Track positions and react to onchain activity in real time across networks.

* [Balances](/docs/reference/token-api-overview): token balances for an address.
* [DeFi](/docs/reference/portfolio-apis): DeFi positions for an address (beta).
* [Activity](/docs/reference/transfers-api-quickstart): trade and transfer history for an address.
* [Webhooks](/docs/reference/webhooks-overview): get notified as soon as trades and deposits land.
* [Gas Sponsorship](/docs/wallets/low-level-infra/gas-manager/policy-management/api-endpoints): pay gas so users can trade without holding native tokens.
