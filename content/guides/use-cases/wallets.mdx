Show users everything they own across networks and keep it up to date.

* [Balances](/docs/reference/token-api-overview): token balances for an address.
* [Collectibles](/docs/reference/nft-api-overview): ERC721 and ERC1155 tokens owned by an address.
* [Activity](/docs/reference/transfers-api-quickstart): transaction history for an address.
* [Webhooks](/docs/reference/webhooks-overview): get notified when an address receives or sends funds.
* [Gas Sponsorship](/docs/wallets/low-level-infra/gas-manager/policy-management/api-endpoints): pay gas on behalf of your users.
