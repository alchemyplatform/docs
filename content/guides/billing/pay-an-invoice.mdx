---
title: "Pay an Unpaid Invoice"
description: "Settle a failed payment and get back to your plan."
subtitle: "Settle a failed payment and get back to your plan."
slug: guides/billing/pay-an-invoice
---

1. In the dashboard, open **Billing** → **Invoices**.
2. Find the invoice marked **Unpaid**.
3. If your card failed, [update your payment method](/docs/guides/billing/update-payment-method) first.
4. Select **Pay now**.

Once paid, your account returns to its plan. See [Invoices](/docs/documentation/pricing/billing/invoices#if-a-payment-fails) for how failed payments are handled.
