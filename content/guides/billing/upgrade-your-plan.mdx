1. In the dashboard, open **Billing**.
2. Select **Upgrade** on the Pay as You Go plan.
3. [Add a payment method](/docs/guides/billing/update-payment-method).
4. Confirm. Your new [throughput limits](/docs/reference/throughput) apply right away.

<Tip>
Set [limits](/docs/how-to-set-usage-limits-and-alerts-for-your-account) and [alerts](/docs/dashboard-alerts) after upgrading so usage doesn't surprise you.
</Tip>

Need custom limits or a contract? [Contact us](mailto:support@alchemy.com) about Enterprise.
