title: "Download Invoices"
description: "Get PDF invoices for your records or finance team."
subtitle: "Get PDF invoices for your records or finance team."
slug: guides/billing/download-invoices
---

1. In the dashboard, open **Billing** → **Invoices**.
2. Select an invoice.
3. Select **Download PDF**.

For usage data behind an invoice, see [Usage Exports](/docs/documentation/pricing/usage/usage-exports).
