---
title: "Update a Payment Method"
description: "Add, change or remove the card or crypto wallet you pay with."
subtitle: "Add, change or remove the card or crypto wallet you pay with."
slug: guides/billing/update-payment-method
---

1. In the dashboard, open **Billing** → **Payment Methods**.
2. Select **Add payment method** and choose card or crypto.
3. Enter the details and save.
4. Set it as your default, then remove the old one if you no longer need it.

<Note>
When you add a card, we place a temporary \$5 hold to verify it. It isn't a charge.
</Note>

See [Payment Methods](/docs/documentation/pricing/billing/payment-methods) for what you can pay with.
