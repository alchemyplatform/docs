Alchemy Smart Wallets are supported on all major chains!

To send transactions and sponsor gas, we provide **Bundler API** and **Gas Manager API** on the networks in the table below. You can use [multiple chains at once](/docs/wallets/recipes/multi-chain-setup) in the same app.

Import chains from `@account-kit/infra`.

## Need another chain?

We're expanding support and can add new chains on request.

<Tip>
  Reach out to [wallets@alchemy.com](mailto:wallets@alchemy.com) or use this [request form](https://alchemy.chilipiper.com/router/wallet-services-chain-support-requests).
</Tip>
